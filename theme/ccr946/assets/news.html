EU Election Debate comes to Claremorris

Published 20 May 2024

Thirteen candidates standing for the Midlands-North-West constituency faced questions from voters at a European election debate held in the McWilliam Park Hotel, Claremorris.

The event, organised ahead of the June poll, gave local residents the chance to put questions directly to candidates on issues ranging from farming and rural transport to housing and EU funding for community projects.

On air tonight

Claremorris Community Radio's Anthony McNicholas was on hand to record the evening, and brings listeners a special report on the debate, broadcasting tonight at 9pm on 94.6FM.

"It's a rare chance to have this many candidates in the same room, answering directly to the people who'll be voting for them." — from the floor at the McWilliam Park Hotel

The report will cover the main talking points raised on the night, along with reaction from attendees. Listeners who missed the live debate are encouraged to tune in for the highlights.
